BirdBot

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This policy explains what data BirdBot collects, why we collect it, and how it is handled. "BirdBot" means the Discord bot and its companion website (the settings panel).

1. Data We Collect

When you use the bot in a Discord server, we store the minimum data needed to run the game and its features:

  • Discord user ID and the servers you use the bot in.
  • Data you generate while playing, such as caught birds, catches count and timing, balance (BirdCoin), achievements, BirdPass progress, powerups, cage contents, trade/fight/gamble records, and redemption history.
  • Server settings, including which channel to use and any custom modifiers configured through the panel.

When you use the settings panel, we additionally store:

  • Your Discord user ID and username.
  • A login session cookie, so you stay signed in.

2. How You Sign In to the Panel

Sign-in uses Discord's OAuth2 flow. We request the identify scope (to show who you are) and the guilds scope (to list servers you can manage). We check that logins happen through Discord; we never receive or store your Discord password. We do not request or store your email, and we do not request message content.

3. Cookies and Sessions

The panel sets a secure, HTTP-only session cookie named birdbot_session. It lets you stay signed in and expires automatically. It does not contain your Discord password, and it is not readable by client-side scripts.

4. Why We Collect Data

  • To run the bot's features: spawns, inventories, trades, fights, gambling, cages, BirdPass, shop purchases, and leaderboards.
  • To apply per-server settings from the panel.
  • To block cheaters and abuse (for example, auto-catching detection and bans).
  • To improve reliability and troubleshoot issues.

5. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell or rent your data. Data is processed by the infrastructure providers that run the service (such as the hosting provider and database provider) solely to operate it. Discord may also process your data according to Discord's own policies, since the bot lives inside Discord.

6. How Long We Keep Data

Game and leaderboard data is kept for as long as the bot runs and is available on that server. Session data expires automatically. Login sessions expire after a limited time. If you want anything deleted, contact us at the address below.

7. Deleting Your Data

You can remove BirdBot from a server you manage, which stops data collection in that server. For deletion of your stored data, contact us through the project's GitHub repository, and we will remove what we can.

8. Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date at the top reflects the latest revision. Continuing to use the service after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about your data? Reach out through the project's GitHub repository: github.com/realxquerzy-ops/BirdBot.
