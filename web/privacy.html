Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This is the short version, hopefully in plain English: we store what's needed to run the game, we don't sell anything, and we can't see your Discord password.

1. What we store

When you use the bot: your Discord ID, the servers you play in, your caught birds, catch times, BirdCoin balance, achievements, BirdPass progress, powerups, cage contents, and trade/fight/gamble history. Basically your in-game stuff, so the game works.

When you use this panel: your Discord ID and username, plus a session cookie so you stay logged in.

2. How login works

You sign in through Discord's OAuth. We ask for your ID (so we know who you are) and your server list (so we can show you buttons for the servers you can manage). We don't get your email, we don't read your messages, and we definitely don't see your password.

3. Cookies

The panel sets one session cookie (birdbot_session) so you don't have to re-login every 30 seconds. It expires on its own. Nothing creepy.

4. Why we keep it

So your birds don't vanish, leaderboards make sense, and cheaters/auto-catchers get caught. That's the whole reason.

5. Who sees it

Nobody except the servers/hosting that run the bot. We don't sell or rent your data, and we're not interested in marketing you things.

6. How long we keep it

While the bot runs in your server. Sessions expire on their own. If you want your stuff deleted, just ask (see below) or remove the bot and we clean up what we can.

7. Changes

If we change this, the date at the top updates and the new rules apply going forward. Easy.

7. Contact

Questions or want your data removed? Ask the server owner or staff where the bot is used, and they'll point you to the right person.
